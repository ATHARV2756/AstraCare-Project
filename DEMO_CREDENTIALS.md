## How to Sign In

1. Open the AstraCare AI application (`http://localhost:3000`).
2. Click **Log In** in the top navigation header or on the landing page hero section.
3. In the authentication modal:
   - **Via Email:** Choose the **Email** tab, enter the email address and password from the table above, then click **Sign In to Portal**.
   - **Via Phone OTP:** Choose the **Phone OTP** tab, enter the registered phone number, click **Send Verification OTP**, and enter `123456` (or any 6-digit test code).
